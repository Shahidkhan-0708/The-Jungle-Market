# TorchVision

> **Purpose:** Pretrained lightweight classification models + transforms.

## Category
**MobileNetV3-Large**
- product crop → top-k category + calibrated confidence

## Material
**MobileNetV3-Large or EfficientNet-B0**
- multi-label
- sigmoid probabilities
- `BCEWithLogitsLoss`

Initial material examples:
`bamboo, wood, clay, cotton, jute, metal, leather`

## Do not
- use single-softmax material classification for mixed-material products
